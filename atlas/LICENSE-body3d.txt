The 3D body mesh in body3d.bin is derived from the MakeHuman base mesh
(makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj, "body" group only, triangulated, with
computed smooth normals), from https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman

The MakeHuman project explicitly released this asset under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain dedication) in September 2020:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

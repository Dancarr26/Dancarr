The organ, vessel, nerve, muscle, lymph-node and bone meshes in body3d-organs.js are derived from
Z-Anatomy, the open-source 3D anatomy atlas: https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy (models by the
Z-Anatomy project, Unity/FBX packaging by Lluís Vinent Juanico).

Z-Anatomy is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence
(CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The meshes here are an adaptation: grouped into named structures, re-fitted onto the MakeHuman base
mesh by landmarks and limb chains, decimated, and re-packed for the Bedside Assessment Atlas. In line
with the share-alike condition, this adapted mesh pack (body3d-organs.js) is also made available under
CC BY-SA 4.0. It is a teaching aid, not a faithful anatomical dataset, and must not be used for
clinical decisions.
